

## About us 

**The Nova Finance Club (NFC)** is a student club founded at the NOVA School of Science and Technology (NOVA FCT) with the goal of promoting financial literacy among students. The club focuses on providing a solid foundation in finance for academic, professional, and personal success by sharing knowledge about investments, financial markets, and personal finance.
